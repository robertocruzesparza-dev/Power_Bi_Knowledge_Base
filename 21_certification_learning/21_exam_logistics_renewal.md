## Purpose

This file explains the practical rules around taking and keeping a Microsoft certification. It applies to PL-300, DP-600, and DP-700.

## Key facts

- A score of 700 or higher passes. Fact.
- Associate, expert, and specialty certifications expire annually. Fundamentals certifications do not expire. Fact.
- Renewal is free. You pass a short, unproctored, open-book online assessment on Microsoft Learn. Fact.
- The renewal window is six months before expiry. Passing extends the certification one year from the expiration date. Fact.
- You may retake the renewal assessment as often as needed before expiry. Fact.
- After a failed proctored exam you can retake after 24 hours. Later waits vary. Fact.
- Exams are delivered through Pearson VUE. Fact.
- Microsoft recommends registering with a personal Microsoft account. Work or school accounts lose exam records if you leave the organization. Fact.
- Accommodations, such as extra time, can be requested. Localized exams lag English by about eight weeks; if your language is not updated, you can request 30 extra minutes. Fact.

## Steps to renew

1. Link your Microsoft Learn profile to your certification profile.
2. Open your profile and look for the Renew button once you are inside the six-month window.
3. Study the free renewal modules, then take the assessment.
4. Check the new expiry date on your transcript.

## Scheduling advice

- Take the practice assessment first; it is free.
- Check the study guide date. If an update is within weeks, study the new list.
- Allow time for the certificate to appear. Learner posts on Microsoft Q&A report waits of a day or more. Community report.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the credential lapse. A Microsoft Q&A moderator states that DP-203 holders cannot renew because the exam retired (community report; see the DP-203 study guide banner for the retirement date).
- Using a work account as the only login for exam records.

## Limits and version notes

Renewal rules and windows may change. Confirm on the renewal page before relying on dates.
