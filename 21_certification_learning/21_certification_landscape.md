## Purpose

This file lists the Microsoft exams a Power BI or Fabric practitioner is likely to consider. It records exam code, credential name, level, and status.

## Key facts

- Associate-level credentials expire after 12 months unless renewed. Fundamentals credentials do not expire. Fact.
- The Azure Data Engineer exam DP-203 retired on 2025-03-31. Microsoft's moderators point to DP-700 as the closest replacement. Fact (retirement); Inference (replacement fit).
- DP-600 and DP-700 both change on 2026-10-19 in English. Fact.

## Comparison table

| Exam | Credential | Level | Focus | Status on 2026-10-07 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PL-300 | Power BI Data Analyst Associate | Intermediate | Prepare, model, visualize, secure Power BI | Active, skills as of 2026-04-20 |
| DP-600 | Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate | Intermediate | Semantic models, lakehouses, warehouses, SQL, KQL, DAX | Active, update due 2026-10-19 |
| DP-700 | Fabric Data Engineer Associate | Intermediate | Ingestion, transformation, orchestration, monitoring in Fabric | Active, update due 2026-10-19 |
| DP-900 | Azure Data Fundamentals | Beginner | Core data concepts, includes Power BI and Fabric basics | Active, skills as of 2026-07-21 |

DP-700 weights: implement and manage an analytics solution 30-35%; ingest and transform data 30-35%; monitor and optimize 30-35%. Fact, from the post-update study guide snippet.

DP-900 weights: core data concepts 25-30%; relational data 20-25%; non-relational data 15-20%; analytics workloads 25-30%. The analytics area now names Fabric and Power BI data visualization. Fact.

## Limits and version notes

- Exam pages show no price. Microsoft's FAQ says associate and expert exams typically cost US$165, adjusted by country, plus possible taxes. Check the scheduling page for your amount [S2611].
- Other Microsoft exams in the Fabric orbit may exist. Only the four above were verified. Unknown for others.
- DP-700 study guide read in full on 2026-10-08. It shows skills as of 2026-10-19 with weights 30-35% each; only the workspace settings and optimize performance groups changed, both minor [S2612].
