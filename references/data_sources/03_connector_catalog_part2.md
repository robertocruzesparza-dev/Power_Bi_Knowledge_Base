# Connector catalog (part 2 of 3)

Purpose: find whether a connector exists and where it runs. Each line shows the name, the publisher, then three flags in this order: Power BI semantic models (SM), Power BI dataflows Gen1 (DF1), Fabric Dataflow Gen2 (DF2). Y means the official index marks it supported, N means not supported. Beta or Preview in a name means limited support. Text was parsed from the official index on 2026-10-07 and a few names may be imperfect. Capabilities such as DirectQuery are not in the index; see the [connector capability details](03_connector_capability_details.md).
