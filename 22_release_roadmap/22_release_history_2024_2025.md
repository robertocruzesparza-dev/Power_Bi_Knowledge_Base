# Monthly release history, October 2024 to December 2025

Purpose: see what changed and when, for questions such as "when did feature X arrive". Source: Microsoft's archive of monthly updates, read on 2026-10-07. Highlights are paraphrased and selective; the monthly blog post has full detail.

## How to read this

- Version is the Power BI Desktop build number shown in the archive heading.
- Status words (preview, GA) are as stated in that month's notes. Later changes are in [2026 history](22_release_history_2026.md).
- No December update appears in the archive for 2024 or 2025 (Fact, S336).

## 2024

| Month | Desktop version | Highlights |
|---|---|---|

| Nov 2024 | 2.138.782.0 | Copilot summaries in subscriptions (preview), text slicer (preview), small multiples for new card visual (preview), Azure Maps path layer, alerts with Activator GA |

## Things to know

- Desktop builds are not serviced after release; always use the latest, and files saved in newer builds may not open in older ones (Fact, S336).
- Only English Desktop versions are archived (Fact, S336).
